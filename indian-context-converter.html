✦ About This Tool

Why we built the Indian Context Converter

Global unit converters know metres and kilograms — they've never heard of a bigha, a tola, or a lakh. But land deeds in most Indian states are still measured in bigha or gunta, jewellers weigh gold in tola, grocers sell in pav and chhatak, and every Indian salary slip and property price is written in lakhs and crores rather than millions. This tool covers the everyday conversions — land, weight, volume, length, and Indian number formatting — that international calculators simply get wrong or don't offer at all.

Each converter uses the regional conversion factor most commonly cited for that unit (bigha varies by state, so a note flags that), and the Indian numbering card turns any number into its lakh/crore form and back, plus spells it out in words the way it'd appear on a cheque.
