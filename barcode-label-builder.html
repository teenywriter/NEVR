✦ About This Tool

Why we built the A4 Barcode Label Builder

Small retailers and warehouse teams in India rarely need a dedicated label printer — they need a page of accurate barcode stickers on the A4 sheets they already buy in bulk. This tool takes a pasted SKU list (straight from Excel or Google Sheets, tab-separated and all), lays it out against the exact millimetre geometry of common Indian sticker sheet brands, and renders a print-ready page of Code 128, EAN-13, Code 39, UPC-A, or ITF-14 barcodes.

Getting the margins and gutters right is the entire game with die-cut label sheets — a print that's off by a millimetre means every barcode lands slightly on the cut line. The built-in presets encode the real-world measurements for popular sheet formats, and everything renders live so you can see exactly how a page will look before it touches your printer.
