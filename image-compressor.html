✦ About This Tool

Why we built the Govt-Spec Image Compressor

Every Indian government form — passport, PAN, UPSC, SSC, university admissions — has its own exact photo and signature size limits, usually specified as a KB range and pixel dimensions that no phone camera or scanner ever produces by default. This tool exists for the very specific, very common frustration of a form rejecting your upload because your photo is "too large" or "too small."

Pick a preset — or set a custom target — and the tool resizes and re-compresses the image directly in your browser using canvas re-encoding, iterating on quality until the file lands inside the required KB range while keeping dimensions correct. A crop tool is included for passport/photo-ID aspect ratios.
