✦ About This Tool

Why we built the UPI QR Builder

Every shopkeeper, freelancer, and event organiser in India eventually needs a fast way to collect a UPI payment without printing a static sticker or digging through a banking app for their VPA's QR. This tool builds one on demand — with an exact amount, a payee name, a payment note, and even a reference ID baked in, so the money that comes in is easy to reconcile later.

Under the hood it's just the standard UPI deep-link spec (upi://pay?pa=…) that every UPI app already understands — GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and most bank apps read the exact same format. The QR itself is rendered locally in your browser using a small JavaScript library, and the "Open in app" button builds the matching app-specific deep link.
