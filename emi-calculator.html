✦ About This Tool

Why we built the EMI Pre-Payment Saver

Bank apps show you an EMI amount. Almost none of them clearly show what happens if you make a lump-sum or recurring pre-payment — how much interest you actually save, and how many months you shave off the loan. That gap is the entire reason this tool exists: enter your loan amount, rate, and tenure, add an optional pre-payment, and see the two amortisation paths — normal vs. with pre-payment — side by side.

The maths follows the standard reducing-balance EMI formula banks use in India, recomputed month by month so extra payments correctly shrink the principal (and therefore the interest) from that point forward, rather than just being subtracted at the end. The year-by-year breakdown table lets you sanity-check the numbers against your own bank statement.
