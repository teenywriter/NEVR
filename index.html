We got tired of the friction.
So we built the antidote.

We constantly found ourselves reaching for quick, reliable tools—whether it was generating a GST invoice for a shop, spinning up a UPI QR code, or quickly cleaning up text. But the free tools out there were always cluttered, slow, or quietly harvesting data. The driving mission behind NEVR has always been simple: to help people save time and get their tasks done without the headache. Whether you are running a business, handling freelance work, or just managing your day, you deserve a premium experience.

That is why we brought the full creative power of our own PixelShade Design Studios to this project. We didn't just want to build free tools; we wanted to craft a flawless, beautifully designed workspace that gives back to the community.

The Trust Manifesto

Why is this free? Because we're not greedy.

Most "free" toolkits are just data-harvesting operations in disguise. They charge you a subscription because they don't know how to build lean software. We do. NEVR runs entirely on your device — no cloud, no tracking, no hidden agendas.

 
01

Zero Data Harvesting

Your invoices, salary data, and private photos never leave your device. We couldn't look at your data even if we wanted to.

02

Zero Processing Costs

Because your own browser does all the heavy lifting, our backend processing costs are $0 — and we pass that on to you.

03

Honest, Quiet Ads

We have basic domain and hosting bills to pay, so we run a few tasteful ads. We'll never ruin your screen with pop-ups or newsletter walls.
