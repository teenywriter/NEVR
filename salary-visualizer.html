✦ About This Tool

Why we built the Take-Home Salary Visualizer

An Indian CTC letter is famous for hiding the real number. Basic pay, HRA, special allowance, PF deductions, professional tax, and — the part that actually moves the needle — whether you pick the Old or New Tax Regime, all combine into a take-home figure that's genuinely hard to estimate by hand. This tool breaks a CTC down into its components and shows exactly what lands in your account each month under both regimes, side by side.

It applies the current slab rates, standard deduction, and Section 80C/80D style exemptions (Old Regime) or the simplified slabs (New Regime), then visualises where every rupee of your CTC actually goes — so you can make an informed regime choice instead of guessing.
