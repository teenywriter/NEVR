✦ About This Tool

Why we built the Photo Privacy Scrubber

Most phone photos carry hidden EXIF metadata — sometimes including the exact GPS coordinates of where the photo was taken, the device model, and the timestamp down to the second. Upload that photo to a marketplace listing, a rental ad, or a public post, and you can unintentionally hand out your home address. This tool reads that metadata, shows you exactly what's embedded, and strips it before you share the image.

It works by reading the image's EXIF/XMP/IPTC blocks directly in your browser, flagging anything sensitive (GPS, device serial numbers, timestamps), and re-encoding a clean copy that keeps the pixels identical but drops the metadata. Nothing about the photo — or the fact that you scrubbed it — is ever sent to a server.
